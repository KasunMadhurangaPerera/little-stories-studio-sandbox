Privacy Policy

Little Stories Studio — Post to TikTok
Last updated: 10 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Little Stories Studio (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use the “Post to TikTok” web application (the “Service”). It describes the Phase 1 product: a browser page and a Cloudflare Worker backend that connects to TikTok’s official Login Kit and Content Posting APIs.

We do not sell your personal information. TikTok OAuth access tokens, refresh tokens, and the TikTok client secret are not exposed to the browser.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to creators who connect a TikTok account through the Service’s Login Kit flow and use approval-gated Direct Post or inbox-draft publishing. It does not replace TikTok’s privacy policy for data TikTok processes after a video is uploaded or published on TikTok.

2. Information we process

2.1 From TikTok (Login Kit and Content Posting APIs)

  • Account identifiers and profile fields available under the granted scopes (for example open_id and display name from basic user info).
  • Creator publishing settings from TikTok’s creator info API (nickname, username, privacy options, comment/Duet/Stitch disabled flags, maximum video duration).
  • OAuth tokens (access token and refresh token), stored only on the server under a per-creator Cloudflare KV key.
  • Publishing identifiers such as publish_id and a time-limited TikTok upload URL for an approved session.
  • Publish status from TikTok (for example PUBLISH_COMPLETE or FAILED).

2.2 From you in the Service

  • Video metadata at approval time (file name, size, duration, SHA-256 fingerprint of the selected file).
  • Publishing choices and creator consent: title, privacy level, comment/Duet/Stitch settings, commercial disclosure flags, the exact TikTok-required consent declaration text, and the server-recorded consent time.
  • Session credentials (HttpOnly session cookie and/or a pasted session token; the server stores a hash of the session token in Cloudflare D1).
  • A CSRF cookie used with cookie-authenticated requests.

2.3 Video file bytes

Videos are selected on your device. After you approve a Direct Post or start an inbox draft, chunk bytes are sent from your browser to our Cloudflare Worker upload endpoint and forwarded to TikTok’s upload URL for that session. The Worker does not keep a permanent video library. Transient in-memory processing during proxy upload may occur to complete the transfer. Published or drafted media then reside on TikTok under TikTok’s terms and privacy policy.

2.4 Technical and security data

  • Request Origin checks against an allowlisted frontend origin list.
  • Approximate client IP for rate-limiting the public authorization-start endpoint (short rate-limit windows in D1).
  • Audit events for approvals, hash verification, publish init, status changes, disconnect, and similar Service actions (until deleted as described below).

3. How we use information

  • Authenticate you with TikTok and maintain your creator connection (including refreshing access tokens when near expiry).
  • Display creator settings and enforce validations, including private (“Only me”) restrictions while the TikTok developer application is unaudited.
  • Record your explicit approval and build TikTok publish requests only from that frozen record.
  • Transfer approved video bytes to TikTok and report status.
  • Reduce duplicate-publish risk when initialization is uncertain (held claim; reconcile before retrying with a new approval).
  • Show your own approval history.
  • Secure the Service (sessions, CSRF for cookie auth, origin checks, rate limits).
  • Operate and debug the Service.

5. Sharing

  • TikTok — OAuth, creator info, publish/draft init, video upload, status checks, and token revocation on Disconnect or Delete my Service data.
  • Cloudflare — Worker, KV, and D1 hosting for the Service.

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the security and integrity of the Service.

6. Retention and deletion

This section separates what the product implements today from our retention policy goals.

6.1 Currently implemented

  • App sessions: expire after 7 days unless revoked earlier.
  • Pending unverified OAuth records: KV TTL of 7 days when stored.
  • OAuth state: short-lived (minutes) and single-use.
  • TikTok upload URLs: treated as valid for about one hour after init.
  • Creator OAuth tokens: kept until Disconnect, Delete my Service data, or the tokens become unusable.
  • Disconnect: revokes the creator-app token (best effort), deletes tokens:<open_id> and related pending keys, deletes sessions, and marks open approvals failed. Approval history and audit rows for that creator remain until deleted as below.
  • Delete my Service data (POST /app/delete-data, also available in the app): for the authenticated creator only, deletes creator app tokens, sessions, all approval rows, and all audit_log rows for that open_id, and clears session cookies. It does not delete videos already on TikTok, and it does not modify Muse automation legacy KV keys.
  • Automatic multi-month purge of approvals/audit logs: not implemented. Undeleted rows remain until you use Delete my Service data or an operator removes them.

6.2 Retention policy (operational target)

  • We aim to retain undeleted approval history and audit logs only as long as needed to operate the Service, investigate abuse, and support disputes — and not longer than 24 months from the record’s creation date.
  • Automated 24-month purge is a planned operational control, not a shipped feature in Phase 1. Until that automation exists, use Delete my Service data (or contact support) for earlier deletion of Service-side records.
  • Rate-limit counters are short-lived operational data and are not used as a creator profile.

7. Your choices: consent, disconnect, delete

  • Connect: TikTok Login Kit requests scopes such as basic profile, video upload, and video publish.
  • Approve each Direct Post: you choose settings and confirm TikTok’s required consent declaration before publishing APIs are called.
  • Inbox draft: you start a draft upload; remaining caption/visibility choices are completed in the TikTok app.
  • Disconnect: ends this app’s TikTok connection as described in §6.1.
  • Delete my Service data: removes Service-side creator data as described in §6.1.
  • You may also revoke third-party access in TikTok’s account settings, subject to TikTok’s interface.

8. Security

We apply practical safeguards aligned with the implementation, including server-side OAuth token storage, hashed session tokens, origin allowlisting, CSRF checks for cookie-authenticated requests, rate limiting on authorization start, and single-use approval claiming. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we do not claim absolute security guarantees.

9. Children

The Service is intended for users who are allowed to use TikTok and to authorize apps under TikTok’s and applicable legal age requirements. It is not directed at children who are not permitted to use TikTok.
