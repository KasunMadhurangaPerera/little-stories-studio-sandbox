1. Who we are

Little Stories Studio provides a creator-facing tool that helps you approve and send your own videos to your TikTok account. The Service is operated in connection with the Little Stories Studio project and is delivered through a web page and a Cloudflare Worker backend.

2. What the Service does

The Service lets you:

  • Connect TikTok using TikTok Login Kit (OAuth v2) with the scopes needed to read basic account information and to upload or publish video.
  • Review creator settings returned by TikTok (for example available privacy options and interaction limits) before you approve a post.
  • Select a video file on your device, preview it, and review a SHA-256 fingerprint of that file.
  • Approve Direct Post publishing with a title, privacy level, comment/Duet/Stitch preferences, commercial-content disclosures, and TikTok’s required consent declaration.
  • Send a video to your TikTok inbox as a draft (TikTok video.upload / inbox draft flow), so you can finish caption and visibility settings in the TikTok app.
  • View a history of your own approval records created through this Service.
  • Disconnect your TikTok connection for this creator app flow.

Nothing is submitted to TikTok’s publishing APIs until you explicitly approve a post or start an inbox-draft action in the Service.

6. Approvals, uploads, and status

When you approve a Direct Post, the Service stores your chosen settings as a single-use approval record, verifies the file fingerprint you submit, initializes publishing with TikTok, transfers the video in chunks, and polls TikTok for status.

If TikTok’s publish initialization result is uncertain (for example a network timeout with no definite success or failure response), the Service holds that approval so it cannot be initialized again. You must reconcile the uncertain operation in the Service (close/recover that approval) before approving again and retrying with a new approval. This reduces the risk of duplicate publishing from the same approval. The Service does not automatically retry an uncertain initialization as if it had failed.

Inbox drafts use a related flow that creates a draft notification in TikTok; you complete remaining settings in the TikTok application.

We do not guarantee that every upload will succeed. Failures may occur because of network conditions, TikTok API errors, expired upload sessions, invalid settings, account restrictions, or Service limits.
